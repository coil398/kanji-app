# Kanji selection and evidence

## Included set and order

The app contains the ten characters in the planning team's release order, followed by the two named reserves:

1. 木 — the first short; the English and Japanese Wiktionary glyph-origin explanations agree with the plan's tree drawing.
2. 本 — the root mark continues the 木 sequence; the sources describe a mark at the tree's roots.
3. 日 — sun; the plan notes the center line distinguishes the graph from 口.
4. 月 — crescent moon.
5. 山 — three peaks.
6. 川 — water between banks.
7. 雨 — rain falling from a cloud.
8. 人 — one side-view figure, not two people leaning.
9. 子 — baby with large head and spread arms, legs wrapped.
10. 車 — chariot from above, with wheels, axle, and shaft.
11. 火 — reserve, pictogram of fire.
12. 水 — reserve, pictogram of flowing water.
13. 土 — an extra N5 pictogram of an earth mound. The English and Japanese Wiktionary descriptions agree; the Japanese entry quotes Shuowen, “土，地之吐生物者也。二象地之下、地之中物出形也”

The first ten and reserve order and the one-line English origin points follow `/workspace/jp-learning/validation-plan-shorts-20261122.md`, sections 1-1 to 1-4. Summary wording uses “The oldest form shows…” and avoids claiming a definitive origin. Each record links to English Wiktionary, Japanese Wiktionary, and the requested Shuowen page. Japanese Wiktionary book notes are copied exactly where the plan provides them. Source excerpts are omitted for the twelve plan-named characters. For 土, the JSON includes a short Shuowen passage fetched verbatim from Japanese Wiktionary; the schema permits `quote` to be omitted when no passage was retrieved.

## Access limit and Shuowen check

The planning team’s 12 explicit characters retain their requested [Chinese Text Project Shuowen links](https://ctext.org/dictionary.pl?if=en&char=%E6%9C%A8). Direct page retrieval returned ctext.org's “Access unavailable” / 403 response, including with a browser User-Agent; the response states that automated downloading is prohibited. No further requests were made. The Wiktionary fallback produced a matching quoted Shuowen description for 土, so it is included. 大 has a matching Shuowen quotation, but English Wiktionary also discusses the outstretched-arms interpretation; it was excluded under the no-alternative-theory condition. For the remaining extra candidates, the fallback found no quoted passage that established the required class and resolved competing accounts. The app ships 13, below the suggested 20–30 range, rather than treating an unverified source link or a Wiktionary classification alone as proof of the additional Shuowen criterion.
