Entry (Shuowen Jiezi, verbatim from the Wikisource wikitext): 天、地、人之道也。从三數。凡三之屬皆从三。

Reading of the entry: Shuowen gives a cosmological gloss and 「从三數」 (from the number three), not a compound of two graphs. It does not contradict the 指事 classification given by both Wiktionary entries; the gloss is recorded in the data record.
